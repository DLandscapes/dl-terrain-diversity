Teaching tiles — SYNTHETIC. Not survey data. Not Kartverket.
Generated 2026-08-08 for the DL-TerrainDiversity student workshop.

⚠️ THESE TILES CONTAIN NO MEASUREMENT. Every cell is exactly 75.000 m. They are
a flat plane and nothing else. They must never be described, exported or cited
as terrain data, and they carry no Kartverket attribution because Kartverket had
no part in them. The DEM tiles in ../orndalen/ are the real ones.

WHY A FLAT PLANE IS THE STARTING POINT

The tool's argument is that levelling a site is volume-neutral and destroys the
micro-relief that differentiates habitat: on the real Ørndalen patch, Shannon H'
falls 1.721 -> 0.000, seven species classes -> one, and the one is the invasive.

A student starting on the surveyed ground has to break it before they can learn
anything. A student starting HERE begins in the collapsed state — the flat plane
IS the levelled result — and has to earn their way out of it by moving material
deliberately. The first act is construction rather than destruction, every group
starts from an identical surface so results compare directly, and no data has to
be fetched before the tool can be opened.

GEOLOCATION IS REAL EVEN THOUGH THE ELEVATIONS ARE NOT

The headers are byte-for-byte the Ørndalen ones: same origins, same extents,
same CRS. That is deliberate and it is what makes the rest of the model apply —
the species table is calibrated for Troms, the solar geometry is computed at
69.7 N, and the prevailing wind is the Tromsø SW. Move these tiles somewhere
else and the biotic layer stops meaning anything.

It also keeps the two-scale nest exact: the patch is 16 x 16 cells of the
context, offset 148 east and 156 north, with no fractional alignment anywhere —
so the context cut-out and the graded apron behave exactly as they do on the
real tiles.

  flat75_025m.tif   256x256 @ 0.25 m  = 64 m      origin 654942, 7737700
  flat75_4m.tif     256x256 @ 4.00 m  = 1.024 km  origin 654350, 7737076

  CRS       EPSG:25833 (EUREF89 UTM33N), as the real tiles
  Values    75.000 m in every cell, float32, no nodata
  Relief    0.000 m

WHAT A PERFECT PLANE DOES TO THE ANALYSIS — expected, not broken

  slope 0 everywhere · aspect has NO ANSWER anywhere (correctly NaN, not north)
  ruggedness 0 · geodiversity 0 · one landform class
  wetness undefined or uniform — there is no gradient for water to follow
  no closed depressions, no storage, nothing retained from any rainfall event
  one species class holding the whole surface, and it is the invasive

Those readouts are the honest description of a plane. They are also the exact
numbers the workshop asks students to move away from.
